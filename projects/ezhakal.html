Ezhakal

Ezhakal narrates the story of the Paravur Handloom Weavers Society in Chendamangalam, founded in 1954. The documentation delves into the lives of its weavers and the dwindling craft of handloom weaving, covering everything from the society’s history and the manufacturing process to the range of handloom products it offers.

It highlights the labour behind creating fabric by hand and the importance of preserving a craft that runs through generations. As part of a team of six, I carried out on-site field research and translated our findings into a coffee table book capturing the craft, culture, and lives of the weavers.
