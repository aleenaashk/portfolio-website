Apple Health

Apple Health is Apple’s central hub for tracking and understanding personal health data from iPhone, Apple Watch, and third-party apps.

As part of a team of three, I helped redesign the app from top to bottom, using research, surveys, and interviews to uncover where users struggled with their health data. Those insights shaped personas, empathy maps, card sorting, and a restructured information architecture, which we brought to life through low-, mid-, and high-fidelity wireframes.
