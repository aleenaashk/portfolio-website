Manglish Keyboard

Manglish Keyboard is a smart keyboard extension that enables effortless Malayalam typing through English transliteration, with fast word predictions, bilingual typing, spell-correct, and quick access to emojis.

As part of the project, I led a UX research study to understand why younger users weren’t adopting the keyboard, uncovering key usability gaps and translating my findings into actionable recommendations to improve the experience and drive stronger engagement.

  • Duration: 1 week
  • UI/UX Design
